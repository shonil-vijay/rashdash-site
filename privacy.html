Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

RaSh Dash is built so that your information stays yours. In short: we don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter, including lists, stores, purchase history, returns, receipts and to-dos, is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. The developer has no servers and cannot access your iCloud data.

Sharing with family

When you share your household, the people you invite can see and edit its lists, stores, history, returns, receipts and to-dos. Sharing is handled by Apple's iCloud, and you can see who has access or stop sharing from Settings → Manage Sharing. The name you enter in RaSh Dash's Settings is shown to people you share with, next to items you add.

Requests the app makes

To work, RaSh Dash sends a few requests that carry no personal information:

  • Barcodes you scan go to Open Food Facts and its sister databases to look up the product.
  • Store logos are fetched from a logo service, Google's website icon service, or the store's own website, using the store's name or web address.
  • Store locations are searched with Apple Maps.

These services see your device's internet address, as any website you visit does, under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in beyond your existing Apple Account and iCloud.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No collecting, selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Location, microphone, camera and photos

Location is used on your device to notice when you arrive at a store you've added, so the app can show what to get there. It isn't sent anywhere. Speech is turned into list items on your device where that's supported, otherwise by Apple. The camera and photos are used to scan barcodes and receipts; receipt text is read on your device, and receipt photos are stored with your household in iCloud.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item, list, store, return or receipt in the app removes it from your iCloud and from everyone it was shared with. You can also remove all RaSh Dash data from iCloud in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

RaSh Dash doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
