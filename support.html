Common questions

How do I share my lists with my family?
Open Settings in RaSh Dash, tap Share with Family, and send the invite through Messages or Mail. To use WhatsApp or another app, choose Copy Link and enter the person's Apple Account email or phone number first. They tap the link and your household appears in their RaSh Dash.

Changes from my family aren't showing up.
Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud and RaSh Dash is turned on under Settings → your name → iCloud. In RaSh Dash, Settings → iCloud Sync shows whether uploads and downloads are working. If one phone still shows old data, leave the share on that phone and accept a new invite.

How do I get the list for a store when I walk in?
Add the store's location (Stores → Manage Stores → the store), allow location access "Always", and turn on "Remind me when I arrive" in Settings. The widget also switches to the store you're at.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose RaSh Dash's Shopping List. Tap a circle to check an item off; the switch at the top moves between stores and your lists.

A barcode isn't found.
Products come from Open Food Facts, which doesn't have everything. Type the name instead; RaSh Dash remembers it next time.

Does RaSh Dash cost anything?
No. It's free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Data is stored in your own iCloud storage.
